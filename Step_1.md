### Steps to Build and Deploy Serverless FIORI App on Cloud:
###
#### Serverless Fiori App Build and Deploy means building a SAP Fiori application and deploying it to SAP BTP, 
#### where the frontend is hosted in the HTML5 Application Repository and accessed through the SAP Launchpad Service. 
#### Developers don't need to manage a web server, as SAP BTP takes care of hosting, serving, and scaling the application.
